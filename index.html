6.1 · Right-triangle ratios

Meet the 3 sides 📐

Pick a triangle and compare its sides. The angle controls which side is opposite and which is adjacent.

💡 Remember: Sine = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cosine = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, tangent = opposite ÷ adjacent.தமிழ்: எதிர்ப்பக்கம் ÷ கர்ணம் = sin; அடுத்துள்ள பக்கம் ÷ கர்ணம் = cos.
