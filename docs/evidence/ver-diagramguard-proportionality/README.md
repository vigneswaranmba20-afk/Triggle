# DiagramGuard · Converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem

The lab draws D and E on their respective sides at the actual slider fractions. It shows the dashed parallel through D to F while the fractions differ. When the fractions coincide, the two points share one mark and the explanatory text identifies the equality. The previous 0.025 tolerance sometimes asserted that DE was parallel for distinct slider values, including 0.39 and 0.40.

Measured 15 CSS px point labels enlarge to 30 CSS px at 200%. The preview's ratio and implication are displayed as two math lines below the drawing. The authoritative source is the revised 2025 SCERT English Section 4.3.1, printed pages 173–174 (PDF 179–180), and Tamil printed page 179 (PDF 185), Fig. 4.32.

Chromium and WebKit passed 288 configurations each. The checks cover 320, 360, 375, 390, 412 and 430 px, tablet, desktop and landscape, with both languages and both text sizes. The cases include close unequal ratios, both endpoint orders, exact equality, and the snap control. Actual rendered coordinates establish the division ratios and parallel lines.
