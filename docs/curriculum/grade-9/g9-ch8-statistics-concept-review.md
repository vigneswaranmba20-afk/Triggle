# Grade 9 Chapter 8 Statistics concept studio

The previous chapter offered 30 numbered exercise records across Exercises 8.1–8.4, plus a legacy practice record. Six exercises already had reviewed English explanations of statistical symbols and reasoning. The Concepts section had no authored Chapter 8 journey. The six new lessons link to the existing exercises and keep the question data, solutions, diagrams, difficulty ratings and progress keys in place.
