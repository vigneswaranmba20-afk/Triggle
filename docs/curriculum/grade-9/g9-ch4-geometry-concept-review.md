## Mathematics and language checks

- Crossing angles use opposite pairs 35°/145° and 65°/115°. The independent triangle check gives 180° − 48° − 67° = 65°.
- The rectangle uses equal diagonals. Moving the top edge by 36 diagram units gives unequal diagonals with an unchanged shared midpoint. The explanations apply parallelogram results only when the parallel-side condition holds.
- For a chord in a circle of radius 5, perpendicular distances 3 and 4 give half-chords 4 and 3. The independent check uses radius 13 and distance 5, giving half-chord 12 and chord 24. The midpoint-to-centre converse requires different midpoint and centre points. Equal angle and distance comparisons assume one circle or congruent circles. The central angle follows the arc that omits the inscribed vertex.
- Moving D on the circle gives intercepted arcs of 160°/200° and 190°/170°, with opposite angle pairs 80°/100° and 95°/85°. The converse is stated for a simple convex quadrilateral.
- The construction figure uses a scaled 3–4–5 triangle. Its centroid is the arithmetic mean of the vertices. Its orthocentre is the right-angle vertex. Its circumcentre is the midpoint of the hypotenuse. The three perpendicular distances from the incentre to the sides all equal the drawn radius.
- Tamil terminology was checked against the 2025 textbook, including குறுக்குவெட்டி, குத்தெதிர்க் கோணங்கள், இணைகரம், சாய்சதுரம், வட்டவில், வட்டத்துண்டு, வட்டக்கோணப் பகுதி, நடுக்கோட்டு மையம், குத்துக்கோட்டு மையம், சுற்றுவட்டமையம் and உள்வட்டமையம். Diagram descriptions, choices and corrective feedback are authored in both languages.
