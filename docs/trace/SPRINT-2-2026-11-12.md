## Compiler and independent verification

`trace_cramer.cjs` compiles reviewed coefficient models to a separate reasoning representation and full English exam steps. It computes determinants/cofactor terms, Cramer ratios, reciprocal substitutions and every equation check using the existing safe exact arithmetic. Long expansions are split into minor, signed-term and sum steps for phone readability. Zero determinants, zero reciprocal values, invalid dimensions, violated contextual constraints and numeric overflow fail closed.

`trace_cramer_verify.cjs` uses the existing independent BigInt Gauss–Jordan implementation and elimination determinants. It checks every cofactor/minor, determinant, ratio, inverse solution, substitution, excluded denominator and contextual constraint. Tests include all eight source models, changed-sign/domain/answer/working mutations and 240 seeded systems with row-swap and equation-scale metamorphic checks. This is independent arithmetic verification, not professional teacher certification.

The generated batch passes the existing bulk validator and remains under `docs/trace/candidates/`; its planned student module does not exist. An isolated Chromium test injects it only inside test contexts. It checks exact normalized question text, native math, complete final answers, keyboard navigation, truthful Tamil pending, and 320/390/1280px layout. This is a preview, with no claim of offline delivery of the unregistered candidates.

## Pilot harness and limits

`trace_pilot.cjs` requires 60 unique cross-medium parts, pinned source/ledger/PR evidence, no existing working, no unresolved uncertainty, checked figures where needed, and available independent math/bilingual reviewers. It samples strata and fixes seeded 30/30 assignment before work. The plan hash prevents later exclusion or reassignment. Every failure, interruption, correction and cost stays in the denominator and effort accounting.

The evaluator reports failure-adjusted median effort, total effort/accepted part, quality reviews, faults, calls/tokens/spend and a descriptive bootstrap interval. Adoption needs at least 30% lower median effort, no observed new TRACE faults, no acceptance-rate reduction and mandatory gates. A small zero-fault sample does not establish zero future errors. Synthetic results are always `NOT_EVALUATED_SYNTHETIC`, even if their diagnostic speedup meets the formula. Real eligibility is currently zero; **the real experiment has not started**.

The broker still has one labelled local mock and no live provider adapters. No credentials or funded spending authority were configured. No billable test applies. Paused extraction/reconciliation schedules were not changed.
