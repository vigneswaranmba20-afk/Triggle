## Student and Tamil editorial review

**Genuine student testing is pending.** Gemini’s three prompts are suitable for observing the reasoning without giving the answer first.

1. In the three-triangle picture with three rows, why does each cell total 7 instead of 6?
2. A friend says the squares of the first three numbers add to 36 because the sum of the numbers is 6. What did they calculate, and what is the square sum?
3. Find the sum of squares from 6 through 10 without adding the squares one by one.

The answer expectations are the invariant `2n + 1`, a comparison of 14 and 36, and `385 − 55 = 330`, respectively. **Independent Tamil editorial review is pending.** Both language interfaces were exercised and selected source terms were checked against the Tamil textbook. Classroom outcomes and editorial sign-off remain open.
