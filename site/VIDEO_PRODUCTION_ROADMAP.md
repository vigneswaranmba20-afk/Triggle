## Animation brief for each lesson

### 1. Class 12 — Exercise 4.1 · Q1(ii) (P1)

**Exact question:** Find x in −3π ≤ x ≤ 3π for which sin x = −1.

**Concept to visualize:** Turn a point clockwise to −π/2, then add/subtract full rotations, filter integer k with the interval, mark all answers on a sine graph.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class12-ex4-1-q1-ii.mp4`

### 2. Class 9 — Exercise 6.1 · Q9 (P1)

**Exact question:** The diagram has AD=9, DB=16, CD=12, AC=15 and BC=20. Prove θ+φ=90°, show ∠ACB=90°, and find sinα, cosβ and tanφ.

**Concept to visualize:** Build the labelled triangles from the stated lengths; connect common sides and mark why the two acute angles sum to 90°.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class9-ex6-1-q9.mp4`

### 3. Class 10 — Exercise 6.3 · Q4 (P1)

**Exact question:** A plane 1800 m high sees two boats ahead at depression angles 60° and 30°. Find the distance between the boats.

**Concept to visualize:** Draw the horizontal sightline and two downward rays at 30° and 60°; use two right triangles to derive horizontal positions and boat separation.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class10-ex6-3-q4.mp4`

### 4. Class 10 — Exercise 6.4 · Q2 (P1)

**Exact question:** A ship’s deck is 40 m above sea level. A hilltop appears at 60° elevation and its base at 30° depression. Find hill distance and height.

**Concept to visualize:** Construct the sea horizon and 40 m eye level; show two sightlines and how their tangent ratios produce distance and total hill height.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class10-ex6-4-q2.mp4`

### 5. Class 11 — Exercise 3.8 · Q2(i) (P1)

**Exact question:** Solve sin⁴x = sin²x for 0° ≤ x < 360°.

**Concept to visualize:** Factor sin²x(sin²x−1)=0, map values 0 and ±1 to angles on the unit circle, and enforce 0° ≤ x < 360°.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class11-ex3-8-q2-i.mp4`

### 6. Class 12 — Exercise 4.3 · Q2(i) (P1)

**Exact question:** Evaluate tan⁻¹(tan(5π/4)).

**Concept to visualize:** Trace the tangent of 5π/4, then map the result into the inverse tangent principal interval (−π/2,π/2).

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class12-ex4-3-q2-i.mp4`

### 7. Class 12 — Exercise 4.1 · Q2(i) (P1)

**Exact question:** Find the amplitude and period of y = sin 7x.

**Concept to visualize:** Animate a unit-circle point and a graph together; increase x until input 7x completes a 2π rotation and derive P=2π/7.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class12-ex4-1-q2-i.mp4`

### 8. Class 9 — Exercise 6.1 · Q10 (P2)

**Exact question:** A kite string initially measures 25 m and reaches a height of 5 m. The same straight line is extended another 10 m. Find the final vertical height.

**Concept to visualize:** Show the initial triangle with 25 m string and 5 m height, then extend the same straight line by 10 m while preserving the angle to find the new height.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class9-ex6-1-q10.mp4`

### 9. Class 9 — Example 6.2 (P2)

**Exact question:** In a right triangle, the side opposite θ is 7 and the hypotenuse is 25. Find all six ratios at θ.

**Concept to visualize:** Reveal the 7–24–25 triangle, derive the missing side using Pythagoras, label opposite/adjacent/hypotenuse and compare reciprocal ratios.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class9-example6-2.mp4`

### 10. Class 10 — Exercise 6.2 · Q3 (P2)

**Exact question:** A 1.8 m tall observer is 5 m from a wall. The bottom and top of a window appear at 45° and 60° elevation. Find window height.

**Concept to visualize:** Use the eye level and 5 m horizontal distance to build two triangles with 45° and 60° and subtract their vertical heights.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class10-ex6-2-q3.mp4`

### 11. Class 11 — Exercise 3.1 · Q1(ii) (P2)

**Exact question:** Find the quadrant of 825°.

**Concept to visualize:** Rotate 825° in complete loops, subtract 720°, and show the remaining 105° direction and its quadrant.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class11-ex3-1-q1-ii.mp4`

### 12. Class 12 — Exercise 4.3 · Q1(i) (P2)

**Exact question:** Find the domain of f(x)=tan⁻¹(√(9−x²)).

**Concept to visualize:** Slide x across a number line, animate 9−x² crossing zero, then show why the valid domain is [−3,3].

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class12-ex4-3-q1-i.mp4`

### 13. Class 12 — Exercise 4.1 · Q1(i) (P2)

**Exact question:** Find every x in −10π ≤ x ≤ 10π satisfying sin x = 0.

**Concept to visualize:** Follow sine through successive π multiples and select the 21 integer angles from −10π to 10π.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class12-ex4-1-q1-i.mp4`

### 14. Class 9 — Exercise 6.5 · Q9 (P2)

**Exact question:** Evaluate tan1° × tan2° × ... × tan89°.

**Concept to visualize:** Pair tangent values of complementary angles so every pair multiplies to 1, with tan45° at the center.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class9-ex6-5-q9.mp4`

### 15. Class 12 — Exercise 4.1 · Q2(ii) (P2)

**Exact question:** Find the amplitude and period of y = −sin(x/3).

**Concept to visualize:** Show why the minus sign reflects the curve while dividing x by 3 stretches the period to 6π without changing amplitude.

**Upload video as:** `videos/triggle-class12-ex4-1-q2-ii.mp4`


## After you produce an MP4

Use the one-time `GITHUB_WORKFLOW_TO_INSTALL.yml` GitHub Actions workflow, then upload the matching MP4 directly into the repository-root `videos/` folder. The deployment script verifies its presence and updates the available video manifest automatically. Missing video = no Watch button. See `README_v126_SELECTIVE_LEARNING.md`.
