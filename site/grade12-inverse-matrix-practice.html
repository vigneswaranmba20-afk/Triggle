Find the inverse of the matrix A:

2132
  1. Calculate the determinant: det(A) = 2 × 2 − 1 × 3 = 1.
  2. Because det(A) ≠ 0, the inverse exists.
  3. For a 2 × 2 matrix, swap the diagonal entries and negate the off-diagonal entries to form the adjugate.
  4. Divide the adjugate by det(A) = 1. Thus A⁻¹ is:
2−1−32

Check both products: AA⁻¹ = A⁻¹A = I₂. For example, the first row of AA⁻¹ is (2×2 + 1×(−3), 2×(−1) + 1×2) = (1, 0).
